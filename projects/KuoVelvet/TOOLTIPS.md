# Kuo Velvet tooltips

| Control | Tooltip |
| --- | --- |
| Width | Sets the amount of sparse convolution added to the stereo side signal. |
| Transient | Reduces convolution input during clear spectral onsets to keep transients crisp. |
| Attack | Sets how quickly transient protection attenuates the convolution input. |
| Output | Adjusts the final stereo output level. |
| Low level | Scales the low-frequency signal entering Velvet from zero to two hundred percent. |
| Mid level | Scales the mid-frequency signal entering Velvet from zero to two hundred percent. |
| High level | Scales the high-frequency signal entering Velvet from zero to two hundred percent. |
| Bypass | Bypasses processing while preserving the plugin's latency. |
