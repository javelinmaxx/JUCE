# Compressor characteristics — transformer-only oversampling snapshot

These plots were generated by compiling and running the actual
`Source/CompressorDSP.h` engine, not by drawing idealized compressor equations.
The figures and datasets capture the production C++ engine before the
2026-10-24 change to whole-path oversampling, rather than physical hardware or
host-specific operation. That version used fixed 4× transformer oversampling
and 64 samples of host latency. The current version runs the entire signal path
at 4× with the same latency; see the [whole-path report](../Tests/Reports/oversampling.md).
The
original 1× data remains preserved under `baseline/` for comparison. Separate
[square-wave phase](phase/README.md) and [output-stage](output_stage/README.md)
reports were also regenerated from the current source.

- [Compression curves](plots/01_compression_curves.png): DC transfer and 1 kHz sine gain reduction.
- [Attack and recovery](plots/02_attack_release.png): a controlled upward/downward level step.
- [VariTransient attack/release](plots/06_vari_transient.png): direct 0% vs 400% comparison for all four compressor models, with the 3 dB trigger and 6 dB full-effect level marked.
- [Transformer character](plots/03_transformer.png): waveforms, harmonics, small-signal response, and aliasing.
- [Upward compression](plots/04_upward_compression.png): measured transfer and gain curves for all four models with upward mode enabled.
- [Transformer Even harmonics](plots/05_transformer_even_harmonics.png): waveform asymmetry and even-order harmonics as simulated DC current increases.
- [Three-page PDF](plots/yinyang_compressor_report.pdf); SVG versions are also in `plots/`.

## Conditions and interpretation

48 kHz sample rate, mono, unity input/output gain, 100% wet. Compression plots
use threshold −18 dBFS, ratio control 4:1, knee 6 dB, attack control 10 ms,
release control 150 ms, transformer off, and detector HPF disabled. The HPF
is deliberately disabled to expose the control laws with DC; this differs from
the plug-in's default 80 Hz detector HPF.

Each transfer point uses a fresh engine and two seconds at a constant level;
the final 250 ms are measured. DC output is averaged, while sine gain reduction
is the input/output RMS ratio. Sine input levels on the horizontal axis are
**peak** dBFS; RMS-detecting models therefore respond differently from the
peak-detecting models at the same plotted input level.

The dynamics stimulus starts at −36 dBFS, changes to −6 dBFS at 100 ms,
then returns to −36 dBFS at 1100 ms. DC steps isolate envelope behavior from
carrier ripple. Times below are measured crossings, not the knob settings or
industry-standard equipment specifications. Attack is the first crossing of
90% of the end-of-hold reduction; release is the first crossing of 10% of
reduction at the downward step. The variable-mu overshoot means its attack
crossing is **not** a settling-time measurement.

| Model | End-of-hold reduction | Attack to 90% | Recovery to 10% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FET feed-forward | 9.00 dB | 5.75 ms | 241.81 ms |
| VCA feedback | 9.00 dB | 5.75 ms | 345.40 ms |
| Variable-mu | 6.36 dB | 13.04 ms | 639.77 ms |
| Optical | 9.00 dB | 53.65 ms | 1145.81 ms |

At these settings, FET and VCA attacks nearly coincide. Their release times
and soft-knee behavior differ. FET and optical DC transfer curves overlap,
but their sine response and dynamics differ. Variable-mu has a gentler,
progressive steady-state curve and transient gain-reduction overshoot.

The VariTransient figure uses the same DC step and compressor settings, with
Upward disabled, and overlays the default 0% setting with the maximum 400%
setting. The change starts above 3 dB GR and eases to full strength at 6 dB GR.
Attack is measured to 90% of the final step reduction; recovery is measured to
10% of the reduction at the downward step.

| Model | Attack 0% | Attack 400% | Recovery 0% | Recovery 400% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FET feed-forward | 5.75 ms | 17.71 ms | 241.81 ms | 176.85 ms |
| VCA feedback | 5.75 ms | 17.71 ms | 345.40 ms | 252.54 ms |
| Variable-mu | 13.04 ms | 22.65 ms | 639.77 ms | 528.27 ms |
| Optical | 53.65 ms | 148.90 ms | 1145.81 ms | 1050.71 ms |

Transformer measurements disable compression with a 1:1 ratio. The waveform
and saturation-harmonic plots use a 100 Hz tone at −6 dBFS peak, with
0/6/12/18 dB drive, keeping the measured odd harmonics below the 500 Hz
residual-filter corner. Harmonics are relative to the **output** fundamental
(dBc). The small-signal frequency
response uses −60 dBFS peak and 6 dB drive; it measures fundamental gain,
not total RMS energy including harmonics. Integer-frequency probes and the
last one second of two-second tones provide coherent, unwindowed FFT bins.

At 18 dB drive, the 100 Hz third harmonic measures −15.00 dBc. With a 10 kHz,
−6 dBFS peak input, the 18 kHz component measures −115.85 dBFS. The previous
1× measurements are preserved in `baseline/metrics.json` and
`baseline/alias_spectrum.csv` for comparison.

## Reproduce

Linux requirements: C++17 compiler, Python, NumPy, Matplotlib. From the
repository root:

```bash
python3 projects/YinyangCompressor/Measurements/plot_characteristics.py
python3 projects/YinyangCompressor/Measurements/plot_vari_transient.py
python3 projects/YinyangCompressor/Measurements/plot_upward_compression.py
python3 projects/YinyangCompressor/Measurements/plot_transformer_even_harmonics.py
```

The bridge is compiled with `-O3` (without fast-math) into a temporary directory.
The bridge exports the engine’s latency, which the script removes before
measuring envelopes and spectra. It overlays preserved 1× baseline spectra
against the current 4× output. Outputs go into `Measurements/plots/`:

- `static_curves.csv`: transfer and sine gain-reduction data.
- `measurements.npz`: full dynamics, waveform, harmonic, and frequency data.
- `metrics.json`: measured timings, configuration, source commit, and DSP SHA-256.
- `04_upward_compression.png` / `.svg`, `upward_compression.csv`, and
  `upward_compression.json`: the dedicated upward-mode transfer report.
- `05_transformer_even_harmonics.png` / `.svg`, `transformer_even_harmonics.csv`,
  and `transformer_even_harmonics.json`: the transformer DC-current demonstration.
- `06_vari_transient.png` / `.svg`, `vari_transient.csv`, `vari_transient.npz`,
  and `vari_transient.json`: attack/recovery traces and measured crossing times
  with VariTransient off and at 400%.

PNG, vector SVG, and multipage PDF figures are exported. The source commit and
DSP hashes are recorded in `plots/metrics.json` for reproducibility.
